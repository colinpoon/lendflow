### Task 2 — Identify Common Extraction Errors (2026-03-18)
Completed code-level analysis of the extraction pipeline to catalog the most common error patterns. Full report: `tasks/extraction-error-analysis.md`.

**Method:** Analyzed extraction prompts, Zod validation schemas, merger/conflict logic, scale normalization passes, EBITDA deduplication guards, and document parsing code. Cross-referenced against known edge cases in `scripts/test-extraction.ts`.

**Key findings (15 error patterns across 5 categories):**

1. **Null defaults inflate covenant ratios (P0):** When `ttm_principal_payments` and `ttm_interest_expense` are both null, FCCR denominator shrinks to lease payments only — can inflate FCCR from 1.5x to 3.0x+. Code logs a warning but validation passes silently.

2. **Mixed scale within documents (P0):** `applyDetectedScale()` applies a uniform multiplier to all currency metrics. If AI detected scale from revenue only but debt is in different units, all debt metrics are corrupted. `validateCrossMetricScale()` depends on EBITDA margin test which fails silently when D&A is missing.

3. **Missing cross-field validators (P1):** No `.refine()` or `.superRefine()` validators in Zod schemas. No balance sheet identity check (Assets = L + E), no cash flow reconciliation, no check that debt service > 0 when total_debt > 0. Debt component aggregation check exists in `validateArithmeticConsistency` but only warns.

4. **Classification ambiguity (P1-P2):** Drawn vs undrawn credit facilities not distinguished in prompt (facility size vs actual debt). IFRS 16 lease classification rules not taught to AI. Pro-forma cost savings have no materiality gate. Consolidated vs segment EBITDA not preferenced.

5. **Deduplication false positives (P3):** Guard 1c zeros legitimate non-cash items when <35% of interest (calibrated on single company). Interest income gate for gross fallback companies has edge case where small interest income is incorrectly suppressed.

**Verified claims:** Confirmed `interest_income` IS in CURRENCY_METRICS (agent's initial analysis was wrong about this). Confirmed debt component identity check exists in `validateArithmeticConsistency` at line 993. Confirmed no `.refine()` validators exist in validation.ts. Confirmed profit_margins percentage values are rejected (not NaN'd) by validation.

### Task 16: Remove unsafe dedup heuristics from EBITDA calculator (COMPLETED — commits 1a64f9f, b23f74e)
Two deduplication heuristics in `ebitda-calculator.ts` were silently zeroing legitimate
EBITDA adjustment items based on numerical coincidence rather than structural evidence:

1. **Check 1b** (deduplicateOtherNonCash) — zeroed `other_non_cash` when it was 30-100% of

(usedGrossFallback), and Guard 7 Tier 1 (identity match against cash interest residual).
